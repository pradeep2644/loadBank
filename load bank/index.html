    Our Products

    Complete range of load banks, braking resistors and resistance products for industrial power testing.

    AC Resistive Load Bank

    Simulates real electrical loads for testing generators, UPS systems, transformers and other AC power sources.

    • Capacity 1 kW to 50 kW (customizable)
    • 230V to 440V AC, single or three phase
    • Unity 1.0 PF, forced air cooling

    DC Resistive Load Bank

    Validates batteries, chargers, rectifiers, fuel cells and DC power systems with accurate load simulation.

    • Capacity 1 kW to 50 kW (customizable)
    • 12V to 56V DC, current as required
    • Digital metering and monitoring

    Battery Testing Load Bank

    Precision discharge testing for battery banks, UPS backup and DC energy storage, with live capacity readout.

    • Battery capacity and discharge analysis
    • UPS and DC system validation
    • Digital display and step load control

    Three Phase AC/DC Load Bank

    Combined AC and DC loading in one cabinet for mixed power plants, converters and dual-source commissioning.

    • AC and DC load in a single system
    • Industrial commissioning and FAT
    • Forced air cooling, emergency stop

    Inductive Load Bank

    Lagging power-factor loading for generators, alternators, UPS and distribution equipment.

    • Capacity 5 kVAR to 50 kVAR (customizable)
    • 230V to 440V AC, three phase
    • 0.8 PF lagging, natural or forced air

    Generator Load Bank

    Trailer-mounted generator testing for field commissioning, maintenance and performance proving.

    • On-site generator load proving
    • Mobile trailer chassis
    • Commissioning and periodic testing

    Dynamic Braking Resistor (DBR)

    Absorbs regenerative energy from motors, VFDs and cranes to provide controlled dynamic braking.

    • Motor and VFD braking duty
    • High-power resistor banks
    • Custom ratings on request

    Wire Wound Resistors

    Industrial power resistors for load, dump, pre-charge and current-limiting applications.

    • Wire-wound construction
    • Multiple wattage sizes
    • Stand-mounted assemblies

    Resistance Boxes

    Selectable resistance boxes with rotary decade switches for laboratory and field calibration work.

    • Multi-dial resistance selection
    • Lab and site test use
    • Custom ohmic ranges
